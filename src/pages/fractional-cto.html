Questions

Common Questions

What does a fractional CTO actually do?

Everything a full-time CTO does, scoped to the days you actually need: technical strategy, architecture decisions, hiring, delivery process, and representing technology to investors and your board. You get the judgment without the full-time cost.

How much of your time do we get?

Advisory runs on a weekly call plus async access. Embedded is 1-2 days per week inside your team. Both adjust month to month as your needs change.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I am based in Italy (CET) and work remote-first with companies across Europe. Occasional on-site time can be part of an Embedded engagement.

What happens when we need a full-time CTO?

That is success, and part of the job. I help you define the role, run the search, and hand over cleanly. A fractional CTO who blocks the full-time hire is doing it wrong.

How do we start?

Book a 30-minute intro call. If we are a fit, you get a scoped proposal within a week and we start within two.
