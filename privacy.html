Privacy

Privacy Policy

What this site collects, who processes it, and how to opt out. Last updated 23 September 2026.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Miguel Tineo, Cagliari, Italy. For anything related to your data, email hello@miketineo.com.

Analytics

With your consent, the site uses PostHog (EU cloud) to understand which pages are read. Requests go through a first-party proxy at z.miketineo.com. PostHog stores an identifier in a cookie and in your browser's local storage. Nothing is loaded until you accept in the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time from the Cookie Settings link in the footer. Your choice itself is kept in your browser's local storage.

Advertising

Blog posts may show ads served by Google AdSense. Other pages on this site do not show ads.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can opt out of other third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at youronlinechoices.eu or aboutads.info.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before advertising cookies are used. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Contact form and newsletter

The contact form is delivered by Formspree. What you send is used only to reply to you. Newsletter subscriptions are handled by Buttondown, and every email includes an unsubscribe link.

Hosting and fonts

The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver pages and block abuse. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@miketineo.com. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
