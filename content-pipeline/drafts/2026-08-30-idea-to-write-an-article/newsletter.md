Subject: The AI Bill Nobody Modeled

Monday I demoed what an agent shipped overnight and the room leaned forward. Tuesday I opened the usage dashboard and did the math on what happens if every team gets as productive as the best one.

First reaction: pride, the tools are working. Second reaction, a day later: nothing scales cost like a tool that works.

Here is the part we got backwards. We budgeted AI on price, and price is one of the best-documented numbers in the industry. Published, falling, easy to forecast. That is exactly why it felt safe. The unforecastable variable was always quantity, because consumption tracks task difficulty and retry depth, not headcount. Every productivity win recruits more teams onto the same curve. Success and overspend are the same curve.

This week: rebuild your AI forecast from the adoption-success case. Model the bill assuming every team performs like your best team, then show that number to whoever approved the original one.
